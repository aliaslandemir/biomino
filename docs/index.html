METHODS & SOURCES

Records, with a paper trail.

BiomiNO maps a small snapshot of GBIF occurrence records for eight marine species with mineralized structures. It does not detect species or predict their distribution.

How this snapshot was selected

Norway (country code NO), 57–72° N and 2–33° E, present occurrences with coordinates and no GBIF geospatial issues. Up to 180 API results in each of five 3° latitude bands are fetched per species (900 candidates at most). The bands are interleaved, then the first eligible record in each rounded 0.2° coordinate bin is kept, capped at 150 per species. API order within each band is unspecified. This is a spatially thinned sample, not an inventory or an equal-area sample.

Read the map carefully

Survey effort varies by place and species. Empty areas do not imply absence. Historical records and specimens are included. Unknown dates and uncertainty remain visible unless excluded by a filter. Known coordinate uncertainty above 10 km is excluded; other GBIF quality flags are retained in each record. The grid sums the filtered snapshot in 0.5° cells; it does not measure population density.

Attribution and reuse

Each popup links to the original GBIF occurrence and dataset, with its recorder and license. Only CC0 and CC BY 4.0 records are included. Preserve those credits when exporting. The app code is MIT licensed; data retain their own licenses.

For coral reef research, also see the Institute of Marine Research reef service. That service is a further data source, not a layer in this snapshot.
